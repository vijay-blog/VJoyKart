VJoyKart Account Deletion Request

The fastest way to permanently delete a VJoyKart customer account is in the app: Profile > Account & Settings > Delete Account.

If you cannot access the app, submit a request below. We will verify ownership before processing it; submitting this form does not immediately delete an account.

Provide at least a mobile number or email address so we can verify and respond to your request.

What happens to your information

We delete account credentials, customer notifications, and personal profile data. Order and payment records may be retained where required for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. Retained records are anonymized where appropriate.
