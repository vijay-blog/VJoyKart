VJoyKart Privacy Policy

VJoyKart uses customer information to provide shopping, payment, delivery, and support services.

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the VJoyKart app at Profile > Account & Settings > Delete Account. If you cannot access the app, submit a request at VJoyKart Account Deletion Request. We verify ownership before processing a public request.

What is deleted and retained

Deletion removes account credentials, customer notifications, and personal profile and address information. Orders and payment records may be retained when required for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. Retained records are anonymized where appropriate.
